used. All clips were trimmed from the original and converted to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV
(stereo originals: channels averaged; 48 kHz originals: resampled). No filtering or gain change
by this project (XC593644 was normalized to -3 dB by its recordist). Clips are shared under the
same license as the original (CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC-SA require this). NonCommercial (NC) clips
may only be used non-commercially; Game Call Hero is free, with no ads, purchases or donations.
Machine-readable details, measurements and cited call definitions:
`public/audio/barred-owl/manifest.json`.

## Test fixtures

`test/fixtures/junk/*.wav` (silence, white noise, wind, claps, pink noise, tone
sweep) are synthesized by `scripts/make-junk-clips.mjs`. Not recordings. Released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

`test/fixtures/turkey-other/*.wav` are non-gobble wild turkey sounds (short
putt-like clicks) used only as wrong-call negatives in the calibration test; not
shipped in the app. Trimmed from the original and converted to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit
WAV (the 48 kHz originals were resampled). No filtering or gain change. Shared under
the same license as the original. Details: `test/fixtures/turkey-other/manifest.json`.
